# Manuscript and technical document — 5 September 2026

This release aligns the manuscript and technical documentation with the completed five-seed Malaya attribution-method robustness campaign.

The central scope boundary is unchanged: OFRA produces the prediction, the attribution methods explain the stored registered margin, and ETG records offline governance evidence. ETG does not alter training, routing, replay, or predictions in the reported experiments.

The documents report:

- five stochastic seeds: 1, 2, 3, 4, and 42;
- Expected Gradients, Feature Ablation, and Gradient × Input;
- 82 eligible class-by-adjacent-checkpoint transitions;
- mean seed-level silent-drift rates of 73.98%, 3.60%, and 14.34%;
- all-method agreement of 50.0% for admission, 54.0% for ETG state, and 35.0% for the drift conclusion;
- the conclusion that ETG is method-conditioned offline evidence, not a method-independent certification mechanism.
