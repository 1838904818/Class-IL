## Decision rule for ETG

If attribution/decomposition does not improve the same-action comparator at
matched budgets, remove that trigger from the main algorithmic claim. If the
limited R1 action produces no useful held-out benefit, do not relabel an audit
ledger as governance. The permitted outcomes include a supplementary audit-only
ETG, a simpler error/reachability controller, or removing ETG from the headline.
